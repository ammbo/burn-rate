# Burn Rate brand assets

Warm black `#120e0b`, cream `#f6efe6`, ember `#ff4d1c`, gold `#f0c27a`. The website retains Fraunces, Outfit, and IBM Plex Mono.

- `public/assets/logo-mark.svg`: transparent scalable flame/arch and dot, extending the original favicon motif and matching the social card.
- `public/assets/logo.svg`: horizontal cream wordmark for dark backgrounds, using Georgia/system serif text.
- `public/assets/logo.png`: transparent raster version of the wordmark.
- `public/favicon.svg`, `public/favicon.ico`, `public/assets/icon-{32,192,512}.png`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`: browser and touch icons.
- `public/assets/og-burn-rate.png`: production social image, 1200×630.
- `public/assets/og-burn-rate-source.png`: original built-in ImageGen output; production version was resized with Sharp without changing its content.

The SVG logo assets are editable code-native extensions of the existing favicon. The social card was created with the built-in image generation tool, then inspected for wording, palette, and safe margins. There are no third-party logos or product screenshots in the card.

## Final image prompt

Use case: ads-marketing

Asset type: production Open Graph social sharing image for burnrateagents.com, wide 1.90476:1 aspect ratio (1200 by 630 composition).

Primary request: Create a polished editorial brand card for "Burn Rate", a creative marketplace experiment where four AI agents compete for one brief.

Style: elegant restrained typographic poster, flat print design, confident large high contrast old-style soft serif similar to Fraunces for title and headline, neat small monospaced labels. Match the existing website: almost black warm brown #120e0b background, cream #f6efe6 type, ember orange #ff4d1c, warm gold #f0c27a accents. Very subtle paper texture.

Composition: generous 64px safe margins. Top left small ember abstract open flame/arch mark with a small gold dot beneath its peak, then brand name Burn Rate. Small top right label "AGENTIC COMMERCE". Main text in left two-thirds, huge readable cream serif, exactly "Four agents." on first line and "One brief." on second line. Third line same serif italic in warm gold "You pick the winner." Smaller right third: four minimal slender overlapping offer cards, abstract orange/gold lines and dots only, no fake product screenshot, no metrics, no currency amounts. Thin warm gray horizontal rule above footer. Bottom left small exact text "Live app. Sandbox payments." Bottom right small exact text "burnrateagents.com".

Constraints: render all requested text exactly and legibly; uncluttered strong thumbnail readability; no PayPal or AI provider trademarks; no robots, money imagery, photos, gradients, 3D effects, extra slogans or invented UI. Keep all text well inside safe margins.
